Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

1. Who we are

Waypoint ("Waypoint", "we", "us") is a web application for planning projects, tasks and goals, operated by Adekola Olagunju. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Waypoint service at this website.

2. Information we collect

We collect the following information when you use Waypoint:

  • Account details: your email address, and a password. Passwords are stored only as a bcrypt hash; we never store or see your password in plain text.
  • Profile details: your display name and tagline.
  • Financial planning fields: your currency and any financial target fields you enter, such as a target net worth and target date.
  • Your content: all projects, tasks, goals and related content you enter, including titles, descriptions, notes, dates, status, priority, checklists, tags, comments, project chat messages, and any files you upload as attachments.
  • Notification data: push notification subscription identifiers from your browser or device (used to send push reminders), and your notification preferences.
  • Activity data: timestamps such as when you were last active and the timing of your streaks and check-ins.
  • Google Drive credentials: only if you choose to connect Google Drive for backup, the credentials needed to access your Drive for that purpose.

3. Signing in with Google

You can sign in to Waypoint with your Google account using Google OAuth as an alternative to a password. When you do, Google shares with us the information needed to identify your account, such as your email address and name, as permitted by the sign-in you approve.

4. How we use your information

We use personal data to:

  • create and maintain your account and sign you in;
  • store, display and process the projects, tasks, goals and files you create;
  • send transactional emails (such as a daily digest, activity notifications and check-in emails) and push reminders, according to your preferences;
  • provide optional AI features you use, such as AI Insights, AI project creation chat and tiny-step suggestions;
  • provide the optional "Go Live" embedded video room and optional Google Drive backup when you use them;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Service providers

We use the following third-party service providers that process data on our behalf or when you use a related feature:

  • Anthropic (if configured): when you use AI features such as AI Insights, AI project creation chat, and tiny-step suggestions, the relevant task and goal text is sent to Anthropic to generate a response.
  • Google (Gemini API) (if configured): used for the same AI features as above, with the relevant task and goal text sent to Google to generate a response.
  • Resend: delivers our transactional emails (daily digest, activity notifications and check-in emails) to your email address.
  • Railway: hosts the application and its database.
  • Jitsi Meet (meet.jit.si, public server): used for the optional "Go Live" embedded video room. Video room content is handled by Jitsi when you use this feature.
  • Google Drive: only if you choose to connect it for backup.

Where we use AI providers, you choose when to use those features. If you do not use AI features, task and goal text is not sent to these AI providers for that purpose.

6. Shared projects and collaborators

When you invite collaborators to a project, they can see that project's content and project chat according to the role you give them: viewer, commenter, or editor. Please only invite people you trust, and remember that anything you share with a collaborator may be seen, copied or further shared by them.

7. Cookies

Waypoint sets a single session cookie that is strictly necessary to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies.

8. Your rights and choices

You can use the following self-service tools in Waypoint:

  • Access and export: export a copy of your own data using the data export feature.
  • Correction: update your profile details at any time.
  • Deletion: permanently delete your account and all associated data using the account deletion feature.
  • Notification opt-out: turn off email and push notifications in Settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as the right to object to or restrict certain processing, or to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. For requests that cannot be handled in the app, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL - to be set by operator].

9. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is removed from the live service. Copies may remain in backups maintained by our hosting provider for a limited period under its standard backup rotation, after which they are overwritten or deleted.

10. Security

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. In production, the service is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and keep your sign-in details confidential.

11. Children

Waypoint is not directed at children. You must be at least 16 years old to use the service (see our Terms of Service). We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with personal data, please contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL - to be set by operator] so we can delete it.
