Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Waypoint collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the rights you have. We have tried to write it in plain English. If anything is unclear, email adekolaolagunju086@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Waypoint is a web and mobile app for planning goals, projects and tasks. It is run by Adekola Olagunju, based in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the personal data described here under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Contact: adekolaolagunju086@gmail.com.

2. The data we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure one-way hash (bcrypt); nobody, including us, can read them. If you turn on two-step sign-in, we store the secret your authenticator app uses and hashed recovery codes.
  • Sign-in with Google or Microsoft: if you use it, the provider tells us your email address and name, and whether the email is verified. We never receive your Google or Microsoft password.
  • Profile: your name, motto and chosen currency.
  • Your content: the goals, projects, tasks and everything in them: titles, notes, dates, status, priority, checklists, tags, targets and progress counts, blockers, dependencies, comments, status updates, project chat messages and files you attach.
  • Reminders: the times you set for task alarms and whether you answered them.
  • People you invite: if you invite someone to a project or to Waypoint by email, we store their email address to send the invitation and to connect them to the project when they sign up.
  • Notifications: your email and push preferences, and the push subscription your browser or phone gives us so we can send alerts to that device.
  • Connected services: if you connect Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive for backups, a token that lets us save files there on your behalf. If you use the calendar feed, a private link token.
  • Usage information: the days you use the app, when you finish tasks, when you use AI features, and similar events, linked to your account. We use this to run streaks and reminders, to apply fair-use limits, and to understand, in aggregate, whether the app is working for people.
  • Website visits: a daily count of visits to our home page. This is a number only: we do not record who visited.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting provider receives your IP address and browser details when you connect, and short-lived server logs are kept to keep the service running and secure.

We do not ask for special categories of data (such as health, religion or ethnicity). Please avoid putting sensitive information about yourself or others in your tasks unless you need to.

4. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who process data for us under contracts that require them to protect it. Some are only involved when you use the related feature.

  • Railway: hosts the app and its database.
  • Resend: delivers our emails.
  • Anthropic and Google (Gemini): when you use an AI feature, the text needed for that request (for example your goal and the relevant tasks, and any file you attach to the assistant) is sent to generate a reply. Under their business terms they do not use it to train their models.
  • Google and Microsoft: if you sign in with them, or connect Google Drive or OneDrive, to sign you in and save the backups you ask for.
  • Your browser or phone maker’s push service (for example Google, Apple or Mozilla): delivers push alerts to your device.
  • Your browser’s speech service: if you talk to the assistant, your browser turns speech into text (Google in Chrome and Edge, Apple in Safari). We receive only the text, never the audio.
  • Jitsi Meet (8x8): provides the optional live video room.
  • Sentry: if enabled, receives technical error reports with personal details removed, so we can fix bugs.
  • Google Fonts and Pexels: our pages load fonts from Google and a background video from Pexels, so these services see your IP address when the page loads.

Collaborators. When you share a project, the people you invite can see that project’s content and chat according to the role you give them (viewer, commenter or editor), and your name and email are shown to them. They can copy what they see, so only share with people you trust.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or as part of a sale or reorganisation of the service (in which case this policy would continue to protect it).

7. Cookies and local storage

We use one cookie, which is strictly necessary to keep you signed in. We also save a few preferences in your browser’s local storage, such as dark mode, Focus mode, game sounds and the last Settings tab you opened, so the app remembers them on that device. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, so we do not need to ask for cookie consent.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access your data (you can download everything in Settings → Data and Trash);
  • correct it (edit your profile and content any time);
  • delete it (delete your account in Settings → Security);
  • take it elsewhere (the download is a standard JSON file);
  • object to or ask us to restrict processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent or switch off any optional feature, such as emails, push alerts, AI check-ins or connected services, in Settings.

To use any right the app does not handle for you, email adekolaolagunju086@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first; you also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, optional two-step sign-in, private session cookies, limits on repeated sign-in attempts, and access checks on every shared project. No online service can be completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and turn on two-step sign-in. If we ever have a data breach that puts you at risk, we will tell you and, where required, the ICO.

10. Children

Waypoint is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a child under 16 has given us their data, email adekolaolagunju086@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the way we handle data changes. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
