⚙️ Preferences

🌙

Dark Mode

Switch between light and dark themes.

📧

Daily Email Digest

One email a day (mornings) listing anything overdue, due today, or due tomorrow. Skipped automatically on days there's nothing to report.

🔔

Push Reminders

Real notifications on this device. The overdue and nudge alerts vibrate and stay on screen until you dismiss them, not a 3-second toast that's easy to swipe away without looking.

🔴 Overdue alerts — the instant a task goes overdue
📌 Due-today ping — once each morning
⏰ Focus nudges — recurring while something's outstanding
🔥 Streak alerts — an evening nudge if your streak is about to break
Between and every

On iPhone, Safari only delivers these if Waypoint is added to your Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen) — a plain browser tab can't receive them.

✉️

Activity Emails

An email the moment something happens, not just a daily roundup — you're assigned a task, someone @mentions you, comments on a task you're on, or invites you to a project.

🎉

Celebration Effects

Confetti, balloons, and the full-screen "project complete" page when you finish a task or a whole project. Off if you'd rather skip the fanfare.

🎯

Weekly Goal Check-ins (AI)

Once a week, AI looks at how each AI-created goal is actually going and sends a short check-in — what's on track, what's stalled, and one concrete next step. It only reads status, never edits your plan.

🎁 Invite friends, get AI credits

Share your link or send a personal invite. When someone you invite creates their first goal, you both get 20 bonus AI credits.
