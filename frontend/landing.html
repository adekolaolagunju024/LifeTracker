How it works

From wanting it to doing it, in three steps

1

Tell the assistant

Type it or say it out loud, in plain English. It asks a question or two if it needs more detail, and can attach a plan you already have.

2

Get a phased plan

Review a project broken into named phases with dated tasks. Edit anything, then create it in one click.

3

Stay on track

Reminders, a streak, and a weekly check-in keep the plan in front of you until it's done.

Built for stuck starters

The features that get you moving

⚡

"Just start" timer

On any task you haven't started, a built-in two-minute timer makes the first step feel small. An optional AI suggestion gives you one concrete action.

🔥

Momentum streak

Your streak counts consecutive days with at least one completed task. An evening alert fires if today's streak is at risk.

🔔

Real reminders

Alerts when a task goes overdue, a morning ping for what's due today, and a recurring nudge while something is still open.

🎯

Weekly check-in

An optional short message for each goal: what's on track, what's stalled, and one next step. It reads your progress and never rewrites your plan.

🎙️

Talk to your assistant

Create a goal, change a plan, add or move tasks, or set things up by voice. It reads its replies aloud, and it always shows you changes to approve first.

👥

Share with control

Invite collaborators as viewers, commenters, or editors, and keep your private goals private.
