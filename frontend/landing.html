Built to get you moving

The features that keep a plan alive

Start my day

One tap sorts today: what's late, what's due, what's in progress. It starts the first task as a focus session with your music, then hands you the next.

Your why, when you need it

Your reason, your mentor, the quotes that move you (from the sources you choose) and your music, found in a two-minute chat and shown when you'd give up.

Rewards from people who believe in you

Send a private link and a parent, boss, coach or friend can promise a reward for finishing: cash or anything else. It's marked earned the moment you finish.

An assistant you can talk to

Create a goal or change a plan by voice: add, move or delete tasks, link them in order, set targets and blockers. You approve every change first, and your chats are saved.

Blockers you can see

Name what's in the way and tick each one off as it's sorted. A task can't be marked done while a blocker is still pending.

Tasks in the right order

Make a task wait for another to finish, like MS Project. It unlocks the moment its predecessor is done.

Streaks and reminders

A daily streak, alerts when something goes overdue, a morning list of what's due, and an evening nudge when your streak is at risk.

Weekly check-in

A short note on each goal: what's on track, what's stalled, and one next step. It reads your progress and never rewrites your plan.

Plan together

Invite people as viewers, commenters or editors. Comments with @mentions, a chat per project with photos, files and statuses, and private goals stay private.
