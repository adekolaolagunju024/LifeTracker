How it works

From wanting it to finishing it

1

Say what you want

Type it or say it in plain English. The assistant asks a question or two if it needs to, or reads a plan you already have.

2

Get a real plan

Phases, dated tasks, which task waits on which, and targets to count towards. Change anything, or ask the assistant to.

3

Play it through

Finish tasks to move along your road, beat the blockers in your way, and keep your streak alive with reminders and a weekly check-in.

The Journey

Your plan, as a game you want to finish

Pick a world. Each task is a stop on the road, and each blocker you name ("waiting on the landlord") shows up as an enemy your character has to deal with. Switch between 2D and full 3D any time.

Built to get you moving

The features that keep a plan alive

🎙️

An assistant you can talk to

Create a goal or change a plan by voice: add, move or delete tasks, link them in order, set targets and blockers. You approve every change first, and your chats are saved.

⚔️

Blockers you can see

Name what's in the way and tick each one off as it's sorted. A task can't be marked done while a blocker is still pending.

🔗

Tasks in the right order

Make a task wait for another to finish, like MS Project. It unlocks the moment its predecessor is done.

🔥

Streaks and reminders

A daily streak, alerts when something goes overdue, a morning list of what's due, and an evening nudge when your streak is at risk.

🎯

Weekly check-in

A short note on each goal: what's on track, what's stalled, and one next step. It reads your progress and never rewrites your plan.

👥

Plan together

Invite people as viewers, commenters or editors. Comments with @mentions, status updates, a chat per project, and private goals stay private.
