How it works

1

Describe the goal

Tell the AI chat what you want in plain English. It asks a question or two if it needs more detail.

2

The AI builds the phased plan

You get a full project broken into named phases with dated tasks. Review and edit it, then create it in one click.

3

Reminders, streaks, and check-ins keep you moving

Push notifications on your phone or desktop flag overdue and due-today tasks, a 🔥 streak counts your consecutive productive days, and an optional weekly check-in keeps you accountable.

Built for stuck starters

The 2-minute “just start” timer

On any task you haven’t started, a built-in two-minute timer makes the first step feel small, with an optional AI-suggested tiny first step.

🔥 Momentum streak

Your streak counts consecutive days with at least one completed task, so progress you’ve made stays visible.

Weekly AI check-in

An optional message per goal each week covers what’s on track, what’s stalled, and one next step.

Who it’s for

If you have a graveyard of to-do apps and a list of goals you keep meaning to start, Waypoint is for the gap between wanting something and actually beginning it. It’s built for freelancers juggling client work, side-hustle builders with a pile of half-finished ideas, and anyone who finds the first step the hardest part.

Instead of asking you to plan everything yourself, Waypoint turns a rough goal into named phases and dated tasks you can edit. Then it keeps that plan in front of you with reminders, a momentum streak, and a weekly check-in, so the plan doesn’t quietly disappear.
